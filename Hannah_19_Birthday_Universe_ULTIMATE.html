Happy birthday, Hannah.

I don't really know how to fit everything I feel into a few lines, so I made you an entire little universe instead.

There are so many things that remind me of you: flowers, the sea, coffee, little moments that somehow stay in my head longer than they should.

I still remember the first song I heard from you — My Flower Girl. I remember the first horror movie we watched together too. It was supposed to be scary, but somehow it became one of those memories I want to keep.

And then there was the day I confessed. My internet disconnected. Of all possible moments, it chose that one. I still wish I could go back and do it without the Wi-Fi deciding to become the villain.

I know you're busy, and I know we don't always get the calls or conversations we used to. I just hope we get more of those moments again — more time, more stories, more chances to know each other better.

I love your voice. Your eyes. Your laugh. Your style. The little things that make you you.

And even with countries between us, I want to keep doing everything I can so you never have to feel like you're alone on the other side of the map.

Maybe one day we'll look back at this tiny website and laugh at how dramatic Shero was.

Until then, keep being Hannah. Keep being you.

Happy 19th birthday.
— Shero
