Dear Hannah, ♡

Happy 19th birthday.

I don't really know how to fit everything I feel into one little website, but I wanted to try anyway.

You are in so many tiny things I notice now — flowers, the sea, sunsets, coffee, songs, games, and the quiet moments when I suddenly remember something you said.

I still remember the first song I heard from you: My Flower Girl. And I still remember the horror movie we watched together. It sounds like a small thing, but memories like that stay with me because they have you in them.

I wish I could go back to the day I confessed and fix one stupid thing: my internet disconnecting. Out of all the things that could have happened… of course it had to be Wi‑Fi. 😭

I know you're busy, and I know we are in different countries. I just hope that, even when we can't talk as much as before, you never feel like the distance means I care any less. I want to keep getting to know you, keep laughing with you, and hopefully have many more calls together.

Thank you for being Hannah. For your voice, your eyes, your laugh, your style, your personality — and all the little things I probably haven't even figured out how to name yet.

And one day, hopefully sooner than it feels right now, distance won't be something between us. You'll be there, and I'll be there too.

Happy birthday, Hannah. You deserve a beautiful year. ♡

— Shero
